The other day I created a new GitHub repo for a simple jQuery plugin. You give it the calendar ID of a public Google Calendar, and it builds a nice-looking timeline of past events. That’s right, jQuery is still really useful.

I’ve found the timeline format handy for showing a project’s completed milestones, or even past career accomplishments.
