Write-In Candidate • Yardley Borough, PA

The right choice for Mayor of Yardley

Local Solutions, Not Party Politics

Yardley, PA – October 5, 2025 – Today, Brad Oyler, a lifelong resident of Pennsylvania and dedicated community advocate, proudly announces his write-in candidacy for Mayor of Yardley Borough in the November 4, 2025, general election. With a deep commitment to our town’s historic charm, vibrant community, and future prosperity, Oyler is stepping forward to lead Yardley into a brighter era, one built on innovation, fiscal responsibility, and unwavering support for our residents and businesses.

“I love Yardley,” said Oyler. “From the serene banks of the Delaware River to our bustling Main Street shops, this is more than a place to live, it’s home. But we can do better. As your write-in Mayor, I’ll fight every day to make Yardley the best town in Pennsylvania by investing in what matters most: our safety, our convenience, and our economic strength. On November 4, write in ‘Brad Oyler’ for Mayor, because Yardley deserves bold, forward-thinking leadership.”

My Key Issues

01 • Public Safety

Preserving Our Safe Community: Supporting Our Police Department

Yardley is already one of the safest towns in Pennsylvania, a testament to our tight-knit community and dedicated police force. To keep it that way for years to come, Oyler is committed to maintaining a strong, community-focused Police Department that fosters trust and connection.

02 • Mobility

Enhancing Mobility: Smart Parking Solutions with Metered Parking

Traffic congestion and limited parking plague our downtown, frustrating residents and deterring visitors. Oyler proposes a balanced approach to reclaim our streets for people and commerce.

03 • Fiscal Innovation

Pioneering Fiscal Innovation: Launching the Yardley Bitcoin Fund to Lower Taxes

In an era of rising costs, Yardley must think creatively to protect taxpayers. Drawing inspiration from innovative municipal models like Vancouver’s Bitcoin fund, Oyler will champion the Yardley Bitcoin Fund, a diversified investment vehicle designed to generate new revenue streams and reduce the tax burden on residents and business owners.
